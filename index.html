TRADER EXPECTATIONS

Prediction Markets

Current national political contracts from regulated prediction markets. Bid–ask prices show where traders are offering to buy and sell; they are not survey responses and do not guarantee an outcome.

How to read this page

Prediction markets let people buy contracts based on whether an event will happen. The price moves as traders react to news and disagree about the likely outcome.

60¢
Example: “YES” costs 60 cents

You pay 60¢ for the contract. If the event happens, it pays $1—giving you 40¢ more than you paid, before fees. If it does not happen, it pays $0 and you lose the 60¢ you paid.

Important: People may describe 60¢ as roughly a “60% chance,” but it is only the market price. It does not mean 60% of voters support something, and it is not an IRC Media forecast.

BidThe highest price a buyer is currently offering.
AskThe lowest price a seller is currently willing to accept.
VolumeThe number of contracts traded—not the number of people participating.
